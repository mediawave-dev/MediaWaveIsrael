# Contract: the opener's sea (DECISIONS #62)

The file draws; the page moves. Rive renders the night above the water, the surface's two lines and the haze; the physics (`src/look/parts/sea-physics.ts`) and the opening run in `src/look/parts/Sea.tsx` and reach the file only through these names. Generated by `build-sea.mjs` (`npm run rive:sea`); `src/look/parts/sea.json` carries the same geometry to the page.

## View model (page to Rive, every frame while the water moves)

| name | type | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `y0` .. `y34` | number | each surface point's height in the band (artboard units; the calm surface is at 320 plus the rest curve) |
| `r0` .. `r34` | number | each point's handle angle in radians (the slope from its neighbours), so the curve stays smooth |
| `guideStart`, `guideEnd` | number 0-1 | the trim of the guide line (the horizon drawn before the water); 1 and 1 hide it |
| `lineA`, `lineB` | number | stroke widths of the white and blue lines in artboard units (the page divides the wanted pixels by the vertical scale) |

The points sit at x = -50 + 50 i (i = 0..34), one beyond each edge.

## States

| state | what shows | who decides |
|---|---|---|
| first visit (`html.hero-wave`, set before the first paint) | night; the guide is drawn from the right (0.55 s), the bore pours in from the right (1 to 1.5 s), the physics takes over as the crest meets the left wall | `index.html`, `Sea.tsx` |
| live | the water answers the pointer (rises toward it, splashes where it crosses), a tap or click (a drop) and the page's scroll (slosh); drawing stops when calm or off screen | `Sea.tsx` |
| flood | as the hero scrolls away the lid (night and band) lifts and the film fills the hero | `HeroSea.tsx` (GSAP, one transform) |
| rest | `src/look/parts/sea-rest.svg`, the same geometry at rest: before Rive, without JS, under REDUCE, in the prerender, and when Rive misses the 4.5 s deadline | CSS |
| CALM (OS reduced motion) | no opening, the touch at half strength, no scroll slosh; the flood stays (scroll-driven) | `Sea.tsx` |

After the opening the rendered rise saturates softly (tanh) at the gap between the calm surface and the buttons, so the water never covers the copy.
