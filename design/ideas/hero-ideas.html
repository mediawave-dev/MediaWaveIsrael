MediaWave hero ideas (direction C, link to live)

Cheap ideas before any prototype (site-director stage 2 step 8). The recommended idea is outlined; dropped ideas show why.

1

Drag the edge מומלץ

Visitor sees
The hero sits in a device-mode frame at 1440 with a width readout. A handle on the left edge narrows it; the real layout reflows step by step to 390 and becomes the phone that the rest of the page follows.
From
W02 + W19
Does
Mouse: drag the handle. Keyboard: the handle is a slider (arrow keys, Home/End). Touch: the page scroll drives the same narrowing.
Built with
DOM + container queries inside a sticky stage; width set on a wrapper, never on the page. GSAP on the shared clock.
Reduced motion
Opens already at 390 with the readout set.
Risk
Reflow cost per frame: limit to the hero block, test INP. Drag handle may go unnoticed: the scroll path covers it.
Logic before wow
Nati really narrows every site to 390 in device mode before the client sees it.
2

Width readout as the headline

Visitor sees
A huge "1440" next to the H1 counts down to "390" as you scroll, while the H1 narrows on its width axis and rewraps.
From
W02 + W08
Does
Scroll only; keyboard scroll works the same. No pointer needed.
Built with
Variable font width axis + a number tween on the shared clock.
Reduced motion
Shows 390 and the final wrap.
Risk
A changing headline width is harder to read mid-move; the number risks the stat-hero reflex.
Logic before wow
The readout is the number Nati watches while he narrows a site.
3

The link arrives

Visitor sees
First viewport: a phone screen with one incoming message holding a staging link. A tap (or Enter) opens the link and the client site loads in the phone.
From
W03 + W16
Does
Tap or Enter on the message; scroll also opens it.
Built with
DOM and CSS; the message is plain HTML, labelled as an example.
Reduced motion
The link is already open on the first version.
Risk
A fake notification can feel like a trick: it must be labelled and never mimic WhatsApp's brand.
Logic before wow
Every client really receives this link before anything goes live.
4

Your device, measured live

Visitor sees
The gauge in the hero fills with the speed this very page just had on the visitor's own device (measured in the browser), next to the lab score 96.
From
W05 + W06
Does
Nothing to do: it measures on load. A link opens how it was measured.
Built with
PerformanceObserver (LCP, CLS) + SVG arc on the shared clock.
Reduced motion
The final number, no sweep.
Risk
A slow device shows a weak number: honest, but it can backfire. Needs a calm explanation line.
Logic before wow
Nati measures speed on a phone before going live; here the visitor sees the same measure.
5

Scrub the film on a timeline

Visitor sees
The hero film sits on an edit timeline with a playhead; dragging the playhead scrubs the film, and cut marks split it into the page's chapters.
From
W07 + W09
Does
Drag the playhead; keyboard slider; touch drag on the track.
Built with
Video scrub needs an all-intra encode (heavy) or an image sequence.
Reduced motion
A still frame and the chapter marks.
Risk
Heavy media on phones; it shows the video craft, not the site promise.
Logic before wow
Nati edits on exactly this timeline; it is literal for the video service only.
6

Circle it, it gets fixed

Visitor sees
A first-version screenshot in the phone; the visitor circles a flaw with the pointer or finger, and on release a hard cut shows the corrected screen.
From
W17
Does
Draw a circle; keyboard: a "mark" button per hotspot; touch: finger stroke.
Built with
SVG path from pointer events; two prepared screenshots.
Reduced motion
Shows the marked screenshot and the fixed one side by side.
Risk
Needs a real before/after pair from a client [לבירור]; drawing on phones fights scrolling.
Logic before wow
Clients really send screenshots with a circle and a note.
7

Two real sites, one phone

Visitor sees
The phone in the hero swipes between two real client sites that look nothing alike, answering "will it look like a template?" before anything else.
From
W21 + W04
Does
Swipe, arrow buttons, or keyboard arrows.
Built with
DOM images (real screenshots), scroll-snap.
Reduced motion
Both sites side by side, still.
Risk
Which client sites may be shown is open [לבירור]; a carousel can read as a portfolio slider.
Logic before wow
The fear is a template; the proof is two real, different sites.
8

Live address swap

Visitor sees
The hero opens on a staging address; the H1 is the page itself. On the first scroll the address bar cuts to the real domain and a green dot lights: the whole story in one beat.
From
W12 + W03
Does
Scroll; Enter on the address bar plays it.
Built with
DOM text swap on a frame boundary; CSS only.
Reduced motion
The live address and the dot.
Risk
Small and quick: easy to miss; better as the page's last beat than its first.
Logic before wow
Going live is literally the address changing from staging to the real one.
