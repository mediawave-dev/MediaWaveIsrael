# Copy critic: the SOTD Hebrew copy (2026-10-17)

**Verdict:** close to shippable. The voice is mostly native and dugri, and the mechanical rules all pass. Five P0s must be fixed first: one AI-placement promise, one unbacked "client" claim, one dropped extra cost, one claim that contradicts the Terms, and one consent box with no link. After that, the main weakness is repetition: the service pages say the same fact three or four times.

Count: **5 P0, 16 P1, 19 P2** (40 findings).

Sources checked against: `src/components/pages/Terms.tsx`, `src/data/faqs.ts`, the history of `src/data/services.ts` (commit 0875089), `design/world-dossier.rtl.md`, voice rules 1 to 24 in `design/research/04-hebrew-copy.md`.
