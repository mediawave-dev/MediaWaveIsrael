## Register

brand

## Users

Hebrew-speaking Israeli small-business owners and people who sell a course or a mentoring programme (RTL first). Most
arrive on a phone, from WhatsApp, Instagram, Google or a friend. They are not technical, many have been burned by an
agency before, and they decide fast: do I understand what these people build, have they built it for people like me,
what will it cost, and is it easy to ask. Secondary jobs, kept in the footer and on their own pages: video editing,
memory videos and tape conversion.

## Product Purpose

mediawave.co.il makes a visitor write to MediaWave. It says what we build (landing pages first, then image sites and
sites for courses and mentoring programmes), shows the sites we really built, says how it goes and what a landing page
starts at (1,999 ₪, the writing included), and offers WhatsApp at every step. Success is a qualified message in
WhatsApp or a filled form. Lighthouse mobile at least 95 on every page, AA everywhere.

## Brand Personality

Happy, direct, unhurried (D237). Three words: **clear, warm, real**. The copy is dugri Hebrew with a subject in every
sentence and the customer's own questions as headings; it never sounds like an agency or a machine.

## Anti-references

- **AI slop**: anything templated. In copy: colons, lists of three, the "X שמביא Y" mould, slogans, "הכול במקום אחד".
  In design: metric strips, three equal cards, fade-up on every block, purple gradients.
- **"Impressive" sites that say nothing**: the dots, films and WebGL rounds before D237. Beautiful and empty.
- Invented proof: testimonials, client counts, results or prices that do not exist. Proof places stay empty until a
  real, approved sentence exists (`src/data/proof.ts`, `docs/PROOF.rtl.md`).
- Faces of the owners; client names and links to client sites.

## Design Principles

1. **Say it in the first screen.** The promise, then the work, then one button.
2. **The work is the proof.** Real recordings of real sites, not mock-ups; each site appears at most twice.
3. **Phone first, one action.** WhatsApp is one tap away on every screen; the form is two fields.
4. **One field, one ink, one break.** Mint and green-black, the turquoise logo, the sky-blue contact circle.
5. **Calm by default.** One staged moment at the top, one at the bottom, the wheel's slow drift; everything stops with
   the accessibility panel.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA, axe 0 on all 13 pages at 390, keyboard and screen reader passes. The accessibility panel scales text at the
root, switches to a high-contrast palette and stops every motion; its choices intentionally do not persist. Hebrew RTL
throughout: logical properties, no italics, no letter-spacing on Hebrew. Touch targets at least 44 px.
