# MediaWave logo, the drawn wordmark (D266, 14.11.2026)

> **D267 (14.11.2026):** Nati brought the original logo back to the header. In use from this folder: the MW mark (`mediawave-mw-*`) in the footer. The compact and horizontal files of the drawn name are kept, not in use.

The Horizon disc beside the name MediaWave in drawn letters. The M and W come from the MW logo Nati picked on 13.11.2026;
the small letters are drawn as the same heavy leaning blocks with sharp wedge cuts, and the drop sits on the i.
The MW mark is the short form: the M and W between code brackets, the disc on a horizon line, the words Web Development.

These files are generated from the site's own path data (`src/sun/brand-art.ts`, `src/sun/emblem-art.ts`) by
`node design/logo/mediawave-v2/letters/kit.mjs`. Do not edit them by hand; change the source and run it again.

## Files

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `mediawave-compact-*.svg` | the disc and the name: headers, email signatures, small spaces |
| `mediawave-horizontal-*.svg` | the same with Web Development under the name: documents, invoices, `public/logo.png` (the schema.org logo) |
| `mediawave-mw-*.svg` | the MW mark: square spaces, the site's footer, stickers, social posts |

`light` is for white and the light mint, `dark` for dark grounds, `black` and `white` are one colour (print, photos, video).
The icon alone (the disc), the favicons and the app icons did not change: they stay in `logos/horizon`.

Each half of the name carries one soft diagonal light, from its top left to its bottom right. That light is part of
this drawing; the one-colour files have none.

## Rules

- Never change the letters, the lean, or the space between the disc and the name.
- The MW mark lives in one place on the site, the footer. The header shows the disc and the full name, and folds to
  the disc alone.
- Clear space: half the disc's diameter on every side.
- Minimum sizes: compact 120 px wide, horizontal 180 px, the MW mark 90 px. Below those, use the disc alone.
- On a busy photo or video use the white or black file.
